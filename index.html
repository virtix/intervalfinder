Interval finder

Enter pitches to see every interval between them, grouped into the six interval pairs, with the set’s ID and pattern.

Pitches: letters A–G in any case, suffix # for sharp, lowercase b for flat (C E G Bb).
Semitone steps: signed numbers, each step from the previous note, optionally after a starting pitch (D -5 -2 +5 -2). Without one, the line starts on C.
Separate entries with spaces, commas, semicolons, pipes or hyphens.
